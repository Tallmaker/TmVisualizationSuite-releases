# Notices and limits

A notice line under the chart states when the chart shows less than the full result.

- The chart loads at most 5,000 rows.
- The chart shows the categories whose labels fit, and a notice gives the count that it draws.
- A Slopegraph shows the series panels that fit the object.

A state panel replaces the chart when the selection has no rows, a required role has no field, or Data detail does not match the roles. A category that appears more than once in one-row-per-category data is also a state. Above 500 rows for one mode, narrow the selections.
