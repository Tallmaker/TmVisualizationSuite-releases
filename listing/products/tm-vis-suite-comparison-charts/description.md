Tm Visualization Suite: Comparison Charts is a Visualization family for Qlik Cloud. It answers how values differ across categories, between two points, or across stages. The family has five modes: Dumbbell, Cleveland dot plot, Range plot, Slopegraph, and Bump chart.

Category is the row, dot, or line. Series can group the categories. Value is the measure to compare, and End A and End B are the dumbbell ends. Range plot uses Minimum and Maximum, with optional Actual and Target. Slopegraph and Bump chart use a Stage field.

Set Data detail to match the data rows. A click selects the category behind a mark. A value, a range, or a rank is not a selection. The family does not change the app.
