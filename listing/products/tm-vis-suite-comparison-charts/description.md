Tm Visualization Suite: Comparison Charts puts six ways to compare on one Qlik sheet object: dumbbell, dot plot, lollipop, range plot, slopegraph and bump chart. Each shows differences that a bar chart hides, such as the change between two years for every store or the rank of each department over time.

Click a mark to select the category behind it, and the rest of the sheet follows. The charts read your data and never change it. Very long category lists show the first part, with a note on how many are shown.
