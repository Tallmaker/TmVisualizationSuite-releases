Tm Visualization Suite: Composition shows how a total splits into parts on one Qlik sheet object. Stacked bars to 100 percent, a waffle of squares, rows of unit marks, a mosaic that sizes both width and height, and a flow between two lists each answer "what is it made of" in a different way.

Click a part to select the data behind it, and the rest of the sheet follows. The charts read your data and never change it. Parts that are too small to read are grouped as Other, and the chart says so.
