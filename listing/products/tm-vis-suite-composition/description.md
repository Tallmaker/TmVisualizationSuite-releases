Tm Visualization Suite: Composition is a Visualization family for Qlik Cloud. It answers what share each part holds and how a split divides it. Its five modes are 100% stacked proportion, Waffle, Unit chart, Marimekko / mosaic, and Parallel sets.

Category is a part of the whole and Value its size. Split is optional in three modes and required in Marimekko / mosaic and Parallel sets. Qlik supplies the total of the selection, and each share divides by it. Turn on Parts can be added only when the values are additive and do not overlap.

A click selects a category or a category-split pair. The chart does not select single records, and Other has no selection. The family does not change the app.
