Tm Visualization Suite: Distribution Explorer is a Visualization family for Qlik Cloud. It answers how one measure is distributed across categories and groups. The family has five modes: Violin, Raincloud, Beeswarm, Dot histogram, and ECDF.

Value is the measure. Observation is one member of the population. Category and Group divide the population. Qlik calculates one value for each observation, or for each category and group when Observation is empty.

A click selects the observation behind a mark, or the category and group behind a violin or a curve. A notice states when the chart draws fewer groups or observations than the result holds. The family does not change the app.
