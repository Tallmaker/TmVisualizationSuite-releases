# Selection

A click on a mark makes a Qlik selection. Qlik shows its selection toolbar. Confirm or cancel the selection there, or press Esc.

- A Trace case row selects that case. A Trace step selects that occurrence, and a Trace stage selects that stage.
- A Swimlane bar selects the occurrence value, or the event value when Occurrence is not mapped.
- An Event sequence row selects its case. A Milestone or Journey mark selects its case.
- A State transition state selects that from or to value, and a transition selects the pair.
- A Funnel bar selects that stage.

Scroll, focus, and expand actions are navigation and do not change the Qlik selection. Press Tab to move focus into the chart, and press Enter or Space to select.
