# Notices and limits

A notice line under the chart states when the chart shows less than the full result.

- The chart loads at most the number of observations in **Maximum observations**.
- The Scatterplot matrix and Parallel coordinates state when they sample observations evenly. The statistics still use every observation.
- The Scatterplot matrix shows up to six measures.

A state panel replaces the chart when the selection has no rows, or when the object is too small for all measures or panels. A mode needs at least two mapped measures.
