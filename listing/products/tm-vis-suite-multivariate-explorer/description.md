Tm Visualization Suite: Multivariate Explorer is a Visualization family for Qlik Cloud. It answers which measures move together and which observations stand apart. Its three modes are Correlation matrix, Scatterplot matrix, and Parallel coordinates.

Observation is one member of the population. Measure 1 and Measure 2 are required, and up to twelve slots set the axis order. Qlik calculates every measure for each observation. The other two modes draw one mark per observation, and the Correlation matrix shows the Pearson coefficient per pair.

A click or a rectangle selects observations. A correlation cell has no observation and does not select. A notice states when the chart samples evenly.
