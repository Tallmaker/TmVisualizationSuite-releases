Tm Visualization Suite: Variance & Targets is a Visualization family for Qlik Cloud. It answers how actual compares with plan, prior, target, or a range. The family has five modes: Bullet, Waterfall/bridge, Variance bars, Target band, and Pace to target.

Item is one category and Period is one date bucket. Actual and a reference measure give the values to compare. Reference is the Plan, Prior, or Target field that Compare actual with selects. A difference always means actual minus reference, and the chart states the unit.

A click selects the item or the period behind a mark. Plan, target, and range marks do not select. A notice names each reduced view. The family does not change the app.
