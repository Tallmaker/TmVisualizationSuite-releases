Tm Visualization Suite: Variance & Targets compares actual with plan and target on one Qlik sheet object. A bullet chart shows each item against its range, a waterfall shows how the pieces add up to the total, variance bars show the gap to plan, a target band tracks a value over time and a pace chart shows whether the cumulative total will reach the final target.

Click an item to select the data behind it, and the rest of the sheet follows. The charts read your data and never change it. Items without a valid range are listed in a note under the chart.
