Tm Visualization Suite: Spatial Explorer puts locations on a map inside your Qlik app. Many points are grouped into hexagonal cells that are coloured by a measure, so you see density and hot spots at a glance. You choose how fine the cells are, from a few large regions to cells the size of a town.

Click a cell to select the locations behind it, and the rest of the sheet follows. The map reads your data and never changes it. The map shows the area you look at, and a note under it says how many locations that area holds.
