Tm Visualization Suite: Spatial Explorer is a Visualization family for Qlik Cloud. It answers where the values are and how they differ between areas of the map. The family has one mode, H3 aggregation.

Observation is one located member of the population. Latitude and Longitude give its position in decimal degrees. Weight is the value of the observation. Qlik calculates the weight, and the area color shows the total for the observations in each H3 hexagon.

A click selects every observation in an area. Pan and zoom move the view without a change to the Qlik selection. A notice states when the map uses a coarser resolution or keeps the largest areas. The family does not change the app.
