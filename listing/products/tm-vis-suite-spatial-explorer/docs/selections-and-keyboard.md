# Selection and navigation

The drawn areas are the selection surface. Click an area to select it, or drag a rectangle over the map to select all areas in it. A selection of an area selects all observation values in that area. Qlik shows its selection toolbar. Confirm or cancel the selection there, or press Esc.

Use the navigation buttons to move the view: Pan left, Pan right, Pan up, Pan down, Zoom in, Zoom out, and Reset view. Navigation does not change the Qlik selection.

Press Tab to move focus into the chart, and press Enter or Space to select.
