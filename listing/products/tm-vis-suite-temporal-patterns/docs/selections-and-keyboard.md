# Selection

A click on a mark makes a Qlik selection. Qlik shows its selection toolbar. Confirm or cancel the selection there, or press Esc.

- A Calendar heatmap day selects its time value.
- A Ridgeline selects its series value.
- A Horizon time column selects the time values in that column.
- A Cycle point selects its time value, and a Cycle line selects its cycle value.
- A Streamgraph layer selects its series value, and a time column selects the time values in it.
- A Seasonal heatmap cell selects its time and cycle values. A row label selects the cycle value, and a column label selects the time value.

A **No data** day or cell has no selection target. Press Tab to move focus into the chart. Press Enter or Space to select, and Shift+Enter to add to a pending selection.
