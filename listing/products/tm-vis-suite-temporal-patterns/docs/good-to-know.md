# Notices and limits

A notice line under the chart states when the chart shows less than the full result. The chart loads at most 20,000 rows.

- The Calendar heatmap groups days into weeks, or shows the latest weeks, when the object is small.
- The Ridgeline and Streamgraph keep the largest series up to **Series limit**.
- The Horizon shows averages when the object is too narrow for one column per time value.
- The Cycle and Seasonal heatmap keep the latest periods up to **Period limit**.
- The Streamgraph groups dates, or shows bucket averages, when the time range is dense.

A state panel replaces the chart when the selection has no rows, the object is too small, or the data breaks a rule of the mode. A returned zero is a value. A date with no row is **No data**, not zero.
