Tm Visualization Suite: Temporal Patterns turns a column of dates and one number into six views of time: a calendar heatmap, ridgelines, horizon bands, a yearly cycle, a streamgraph and a seasonal heatmap. Each makes a different pattern easy to see, such as busy weekdays, a seasonal peak or a slow rise.

Click a day, week or band to select the dates behind it, and the rest of the sheet follows. The charts read your data and never change it. Very long date ranges are summed into weeks, and a note says so.
