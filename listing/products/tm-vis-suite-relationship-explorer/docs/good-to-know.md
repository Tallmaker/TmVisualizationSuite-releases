# Notices and limits

A notice line under the chart states when the chart shows less than the full result.

- The chart loads at most the number of rows in **Maximum rows to request**.
- Network, Arc, and Adjacency matrix keep the largest nodes or connections when the object cannot draw all of them.
- When one relationship has more than one Category value, the chart combines the values and the largest value sets the color.

A state panel replaces the chart when the selection has no rows, a required role has no field, or the mode cannot show the result in the object. A small object can show the chart in a larger area.
