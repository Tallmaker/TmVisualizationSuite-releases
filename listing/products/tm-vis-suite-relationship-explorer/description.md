Tm Visualization Suite: Relationship Explorer shows how things connect on one Qlik sheet object. A chord diagram, a flow between two stages, a network, an arc diagram and a matrix all draw the same links in a different way, so you pick the one that makes your question clear.

Click a node or a link to select the data behind it, and the rest of the sheet follows. The charts read your data and never change it. Large networks show the strongest links first, with a note on how many nodes and labels are shown.
