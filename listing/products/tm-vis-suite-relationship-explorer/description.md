Tm Visualization Suite: Relationship Explorer is a Visualization family for Qlik Cloud. It answers how the parts of a network connect and how much flows between them. The family has five modes: Chord, Alluvial, Network, Arc, and Adjacency matrix.

Source and Target are required in every mode. Weight sets the size of a relationship. Category colors a relationship, Stage orders an alluvial flow, and Time shows one snapshot at a time. A click selects a node, a link, or the pair behind a flow.

A notice states when the chart keeps the largest nodes or links. The family reads the app data and does not change it.
