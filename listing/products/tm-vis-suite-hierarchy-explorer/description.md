Tm Visualization Suite: Hierarchy Explorer shows how a total splits into categories and sub-categories on one Qlik sheet object. Pick the shape that fits the question: rings, stacked columns or rows, nested circles, or a branching tree. Size and colour show where the weight sits and which branch it belongs to.

Click a branch to select the items behind it, and the rest of the sheet follows. The charts read your data and never change it. Branches with very many small items are grouped into a "more" mark, with a note saying how many.
