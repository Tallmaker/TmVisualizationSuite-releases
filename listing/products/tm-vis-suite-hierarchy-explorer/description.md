Tm Visualization Suite: Hierarchy Explorer is a Visualization family for Qlik Cloud. It answers how the whole divides into parts at each level of a hierarchy. The family has five modes: Sunburst, Icicle, Circle packing, Dendrogram, and Radial dendrogram.

Each row is one full path from Level 1 down to the deepest mapped level, up to eight levels. Sunburst, Icicle, and Circle packing use a Size measure. Qlik adds the sizes up to the parents. Dendrogram and Radial dendrogram show the structure without a measure.

A click on a node selects its full path in Qlik. Double-click zooms into a node, and zoom is navigation. A notice states when the chart draws fewer levels than you mapped.
