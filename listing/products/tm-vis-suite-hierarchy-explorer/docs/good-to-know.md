# Notices and limits

A notice line under the chart states when the chart shows less than the full result. The chart loads at most 5,000 rows.

- A large hierarchy shows the first two levels.
- Circle packing groups circles that are too small to draw and states how many.

A state panel replaces the chart when the selection has no rows, Level 1 has no field, or the hierarchy is too large for the mode. A saved mode that is not in this version also shows a state. Map fewer levels for a simpler view.
