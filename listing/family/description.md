Tm Visualization Suite adds ten chart families to Qlik Cloud. Each family answers a different kind of question: time patterns, spread of values, comparisons, links and flows, hierarchies, many measures at once, process paths, places on a map, plan against actual, and the parts of a whole.

Every family has several chart types that share one data setup, so you can switch the chart without rebuilding the sheet. A click on a mark selects the data behind it, and the rest of the sheet follows. Install one family, several or all ten. The charts read your data and never change it.
